Which threshold does a Toric number refer to?

Rule

A threshold is a transition of a family of experiments, not an invariant of a finite check matrix. Its identity tuple is

(family and boundary, physical channel, syndrome/circuit model,
 decoder and objective, rate normalization, asymptotic observable).

Changing any coordinate can change the value. The Toric card therefore names decoder regimes without putting one number in the entry summary.

These rows are deliberately heterogeneous. Their purpose is to prevent a reader from ranking decoders or hardware using incomparable scalars.

Other transitions that are not decoder thresholds

Coherent information can exhibit an information-theoretic transition without choosing a decoder. lee2025coherent derives an exact Toric coherent-information expression and links it to random-bond Ising criticality. That result addresses recoverable information, not the runtime or decision rule of MWPM, tensor contraction, or BP-OSD.

Coherent rotations also carry phase information absent from a stochastic Pauli channel. bravyi2018coherent gives an efficient Majorana simulation for declared coherent-error models; iverson2020coherence proves a conditional logical-incoherence trend and explicitly does not extend it to fixed physical noise strength as L grows. Neither result licenses replacing coherent noise by a universal Pauli rate.

Hardware is another ledger

A finite logical error per round, a two-distance comparison, a prepared-code state fidelity, and an asymptotic decoder threshold answer different questions. The 2026 neutral-atom Toric experiment reports repeated extraction and a finite comparison; the Atlas records it under realizations and does not insert it into the table above. Citation corrections

  • DOI 10.1103/Physics.5.50 resolves to an APS Physics Viewpoint. It is secondary commentary and not the primary origin of a numerical row.
  • DOI 10.1103/PhysRev.88.1332 and DOI 10.1143/JPSJ.55.3305 are classical Ising-model sources. They are precursors to the statistical machinery, not direct Toric-code threshold reports.
  • arXiv 1810.09621 concerns measurement-based fault tolerance beyond foliation; its placement near a threshold feature does not make it a Toric code-capacity benchmark.
