Physical realizations

  • reconfigurable neutral-atom array with coherent transport demonstrated

    The experiment prepared and characterized a finite Toric-code state on periodic geometry. This establishes state preparation, not repeated syndrome extraction, arbitrary logical-state preservation, or below-threshold scaling.

    References: bluvstein2022processor

  • neutral-atom array with mid-circuit measurement and atom reloading demonstrated

    Repeated Toric syndrome extraction, lost-qubit replacement, reservoir reloading, and decoded logical-memory data were demonstrated. The reported finite two-distance comparison remains bound to its stated round range and is not promoted as an asymptotic threshold.

    References: atomcomputing2026toric
