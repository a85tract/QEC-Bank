Entry facet

Logical operations

Available gates, mechanisms, costs, and provenance.

  • H transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: one parallel physical-H layer; it exchanges the identical X and Z face incidence matrices and swaps the declared logical X/Z boundary strings

    References: bombin2006topological

  • S or S-dagger (logical convention dependent) transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: one parallel physical phase layer with S/S-dagger selected by the bipartition; the induced logical convention must be tracked for the chosen boundary basis

    References: landahl2011color

  • CNOT between aligned patches transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: one pairwise physical-CNOT layer between corresponding qubits; geometric alignment, transport, syndrome extraction, and correlated recovery are additional resources

    References: landahl2011color

  • joint logical Pauli measurements surgery claimed

    Level: measurement-based Clifford interface · Cost: compatible colored boundaries, an ancillary region, repeated joint checks, decoding, and feed-forward; the logical action depends on the complete merge/split protocol

    References: landahl2014latticesurgery

  • non-Clifford gate by magic-state injection or distillation measurement-based claimed

    Level: non-Clifford resource protocol · Cost: encoded resource blocks, transversal inter-block Clifford operations, stabilizer/factory postselection or correction, measurement, and classical feed-forward

    References: salesrodriguez2025magic
