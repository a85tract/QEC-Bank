Physical realizations

  • neutral-atom logical magic-state-distillation experiment demonstrated

    The experiment encoded both the smallest triangular block and a distance-five seventeen-data-qubit block, then ran a multi-block logical magic-state-distillation protocol. Its published distance-five face incidence is the qubit order used by this constructor; the experimental result is a protocol claim with postselection and hardware-specific gates, not a growing-distance memory threshold.

    References: salesrodriguez2025magic

  • nearest-neighbour square-octagon architecture proposed

    A two-dimensional nearest-neighbour architecture specifies syndrome extraction and logical Clifford mechanisms on square-octagon patches. It is a circuit proposal rather than a hardware demonstration.

    References: fowler2011color, landahl2011color
