Physical realizations

  • thirteen-qubit trapped-ion processor demonstrated

    A nine-data-qubit Bacon–Shor logical block with four ancillas demonstrated fault-tolerant preparation, measurement, corrected rotations, and stabilizer extraction. This is a fixed-distance primitive suite, not an increasing-distance below-threshold memory experiment.

    References: egan2021fault

  • bias-preserving teleportation architecture proposed

    Highly rectangular blocks are combined with teleportation and biased elementary operations so geometry protects the dominant Pauli channel. The resource claim includes ancilla preparation, verification, and correction; it is not supplied by block aspect ratio alone.

    References: brooks2013asymmetric
