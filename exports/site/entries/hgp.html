operator

Hypergraph product

A binary construction that takes any two rectangular classical parity-check maps and returns one CSS stabilizer code. Its physical qubits are the disjoint bit×bit and check×check pairs of the factors; its X and Z checks occupy the complementary check×bit and bit×check pairs. The shipped repetition-factor instance is only a small visual witness of this general factor-to-output rule, not the definition of the construction or evidence for properties of every HGP output family.
