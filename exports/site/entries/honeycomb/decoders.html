Decoders

Algorithms, noise models, applicability, and performance notes.

  • Minimum-weight perfect matching mwpm standard

    Represents detection events as vertices of a weighted graph and pairs them by minimum total weight; the pairing determines a likely correction chain.

    Type: matching · Noise setting: periodic honeycomb detector graphs with repeated parity-measurement faults

    For this code: Plaquette changes populate two interleaved spacetime matching graphs. Correlation-aware matching uses the named parity-measurement circuit and its edge probabilities; the reported threshold range therefore belongs to that circuit/noise/decoder triple, not to the abstract schedule.

    References: higgott2022pymatching, gidney2021fault

  • Minimum-weight perfect matching mwpm research-grade

    Represents detection events as vertices of a weighted graph and pairs them by minimum total weight; the pairing determines a likely correction chain.

    Type: matching · Noise setting: planar SD6, SI1000, EM3, and Majorana-native detector models

    For this code: Boundaries, hook propagation, correlated parity faults, and measurement normalization change the detector graph. Planar and Majorana benchmarks must be compared within their stated circuit models rather than transferred to the torus snapshot.

    References: higgott2022pymatching, gidney2022planar, paetznick2023majorana
