Physical realizations

  • IBM Falcon superconducting processor demonstrated

    A preliminary hardware study measured plaquette stabilizers of the original Floquet schedule and reported syndrome-change rates. It did not demonstrate a protected logical memory, distance scaling, or logical error suppression.

    References: wootton2022plaquettes

  • Majorana-tetron parity-measurement architecture proposed

    Native pair-parity measurements and planar layouts were simulated with Majorana-specific faults. This is an architecture and decoder study, not a laboratory realization of a logical honeycomb memory.

    References: karzig2017majorana, paetznick2023majorana

  • spin-optical photonic architecture proposed

    A co-designed photonic implementation was benchmarked numerically under its loss and hardware assumptions. Its simulated loss threshold is not an experimental honeycomb threshold.

    References: dessertaine2026photonic

  • ancilla-free dynamic superconducting circuit proposed

    Data and measurement roles change during the cycle to remove dedicated ancillas, clear leakage, and improve timelike distance. The result is a simulated circuit proposal with a different detector model.

    References: claes2025dynamic
