Physical realizations

  • routed or non-local HGP syndrome-extraction architecture proposed

    Distance-preserving stabilizer-measurement circuits and non-local resource studies provide schedules for declared HGP layouts and interaction models. They do not turn every sparse factor pair into a shallow geometrically local circuit. No generic binary HGP memory with repeated noisy extraction on laboratory hardware was located as of 2026-08-10.

    References: delfosse2021measurement, manes2025distance, chandra2025nonlocal

  • dimension-jump HGP state preparation proposed

    A single-shot preparation proposal uses a higher-dimensional construction and measurement assumptions for declared HGP families. This is a state-preparation protocol under those hypotheses, not a generic constructor feature or a laboratory memory demonstration.

    References: hong2025singleshot
