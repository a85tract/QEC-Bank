Physical realizations

  • liquid-state NMR registers demonstrated

    Early experiments implemented a five-qubit correction benchmark and later encoded identity, NOT, and Hadamard processes with deliberately inserted single-qubit faults. These ensemble-control demonstrations do not constitute repeated scalable fault-tolerant memory.

    References: knill2001benchmark, zhang2012encoded

  • superconducting five-qubit register demonstrated

    Encoded states were prepared, all stabilizers were measured, inserted single-qubit errors were identified, logical Paulis were applied, and the input was decoded. The reported fidelities belong to that finite circuit.

    References: gong2021experimental

  • diamond nuclear-spin processor demonstrated

    Repeated-measurement encoding, fault-tolerant logical single-qubit Clifford operations, and flagged stabilizer measurements were executed on a seven-qubit processor containing five data and two ancillary spins.

    References: abobeih2022fault

  • trapped-ion QCCD processor demonstrated

    Two five-qubit blocks were coupled by a nontransversal logical CNOT with runtime decoding, flagging, and pieceable fault-tolerance primitives. The protocol is not evidence for a transversal inter-block CNOT.

    References: ryananderson2022entangling
