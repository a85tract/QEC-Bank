Transversal Clifford gates and non-Clifford completion

This page first derives ideal Pauli conjugation for the exact constructor presentation. Fault-tolerant circuits, routing, repeated extraction, and logical error rates are separate protocol layers.

Hadamard from CSS self-duality

Physical H conjugates X_q <-> Z_q on every qubit. Since every face support appears once in each CSS sector,

H^(tensor n): X_f <-> Z_f.

The stabilizer group is preserved exactly. The declared boundary operators also swap, X_L <-> Z_L, so the logical action in this basis is H. The transversal statement follows from H_X=H_Z; it does not require rotating the triangular patch afterward.

Phase from the vertex bipartition

Join consecutive vertices around each retained face. The resulting physical vertex graph is bipartite. Under the constructor's row-major qubit order, the two parts are precisely even labels A and odd labels B. Every weight-four face contains two vertices from each part, and every weight-six face contains three from each.

Apply

U_S = product_(q in A) S_q · product_(q in B) S_q^dagger.

Single-qubit conjugation gives

S X S^dagger = i XZ,

S^dagger X S = -i XZ,

while Z is fixed. If a face contains a vertices in each part, its X check acquires phase i^a(-i)^a=1 and a Z on every support qubit. Therefore

U_S X_f U_S^dagger = X_f Z_f,

U_S Z_f U_S^dagger = Z_f.

The left boundary alternates between the two parts and begins and ends in A. It contains (d+1)/2 A sites and (d-1)/2 B sites. Its conjugation phase is

i^d (-1)^((d-1)/2)=i,

so X_L -> i X_L Z_L and Z_L -> Z_L: logical S in the declared basis. Swapping the physical S and S-dagger layers implements the inverse. The reproduction transcript checks every face balance and logical phase for d=3,5,7 rather than inferring them from a diagram.

Pairwise CNOT between aligned blocks

For a physical CNOT with control c and target t,

X_c -> X_c X_t, Z_c -> Z_c,

X_t -> X_t, Z_t -> Z_c Z_t.

Apply this pairwise to two copies with identical qubit order. A control X-face check gains the corresponding target X-face check; a target Z-face check gains the corresponding control Z-face check. Control Z and target X checks stay in their original blocks. Thus the two-block stabilizer group is preserved. Replacing a face support by the left-boundary support gives the logical CNOT conjugation on X_L and Z_L.

"Transversal" here means one pairwise layer after corresponding qubits can interact. A planar device may still need movement, routing, or a stacked layout, and a fault-tolerant operation still needs syndrome extraction and recovery around the layer.

Why the native story stops at Clifford

H, S, and CNOT generate Clifford computation. They are valuable because the physical action is unusually direct, but they are not universal. The Bravyi–König theorem places every bounded-spread constant-depth local logical gate in a 2D growing- distance topological stabilizer family at the second level of the Clifford hierarchy. T lies at the third level.

This is not a statement that all non-Clifford protocols are impossible. It identifies what extra ingredient they must use:

  • Magic-state preparation or injection supplies a non-stabilizer state, then consumes it through Clifford measurements and feed-forward.
  • Distillation combines noisy resource states and postselection or error detection into a cleaner logical resource; the factory geometry and acceptance rule are part of the cost.
  • Code switching or gauge fixing moves to a code with a complementary gate before returning; conversion faults and decoder side information are part of the operation.
  • Lattice surgery measures joint logical Paulis through a temporary seam; repeated seam rounds and spacetime decoding supply fault tolerance.

The hybrid 6.6.6–4.8.8 surgery architecture of Thomsen et al. and the color-code magic- state factory of Lee et al. retain these resource boundaries. Their overhead is not another row in the static check matrix. Flag-assisted preparation in Chamberland and Noh likewise includes redundant encoding, acceptance, and verification rather than turning a bare transversal Clifford layer into a non-Clifford gate.
