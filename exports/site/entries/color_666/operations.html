Logical operations

Available gates, mechanisms, costs, and provenance.

  • H transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: one parallel physical-H layer; self-duality swaps each paired X/Z face stabilizer and exchanges the declared logical X and Z representatives

    References: bombin2006topological

  • S in the declared boundary-logical convention transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: one parallel layer with physical S on even row-major qubit labels and S-dagger on odd labels; every face contains equal bipartition counts, while the odd left-boundary representative maps X_L to i X_L Z_L; reversing the physical pattern implements the logical dagger

    References: bombin2006topological

  • CNOT between aligned patches transversal claimed

    Level: Clifford · Cost: one pairwise physical-CNOT layer between corresponding data qubits; alignment, routing, syndrome extraction, and recovery are additional

    References: bombin2006topological

  • joint logical Pauli measurements surgery claimed

    Level: measurement-based Clifford interface · Cost: compatible color-boundary interfaces, an ancilla region, repeated joint checks, decoding, and feed-forward; the cited low-overhead layout mixes 6.6.6 and 4.8.8 regions

    References: thomsen2024lowoverhead

  • non-Clifford gate by magic-state injection or distillation measurement-based claimed

    Level: non-Clifford resource protocol · Cost: a prepared or distilled resource state, verification or acceptance, logical Pauli measurements, and classical feed-forward; the cited factory uses color-code lattice surgery rather than adding a native transversal non-Clifford gate to the isolated patch

    References: lee2025magiccolor
