Physical realizations

  • superconducting linear parity-check arrays demonstrated

    Repeated stabilizer measurements were decoded from complete syndrome histories, and later increasing-chain experiments separately tested bit-flip and phase-flip suppression. These are one-sector, protocol- and decoder-specific results rather than arbitrary-state quantum memories.

    References: kelly2015preservation, chen2021repetitive

  • trapped-ion repeated-correction register demonstrated

    Repeated correction cycles were implemented on a small ion register. The demonstration establishes closed protocol repetition at fixed size; it does not by itself supply a growing-family threshold.

    References: schindler2011repetitive

  • semiconductor spin-qubit registers demonstrated

    Small spin-qubit blocks demonstrated bit-flip correction and a phase-flip-code implementation. Their control, readout, and noise-bias assumptions remain part of the realization claim.

    References: takeda2022silicon, vanriggelen2022phaseflip

  • split surface-code superconducting layout demonstrated

    A surface-code patch was split into two distance-three repetition blocks and used for a joint logical operation. This demonstrates the merge/split protocol and its decoding stack, not an operation native to an isolated chain with no ancillary geometry.

    References: besedin2025repetition
