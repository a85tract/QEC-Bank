Physical realizations

  • five-qubit trapped-ion register demonstrated

    Four data ions plus an ancilla demonstrated fault-tolerant preparation, syndrome extraction, and logical operations for one protected logical qubit. The second encoded degree of freedom was treated as a gauge qubit, so the experiment is not evidence that both logical qubits were protected by the same protocol.

    References: linke2017faulttolerant

  • five-qubit superconducting transmon register demonstrated

    Fault-tolerant state preparation was demonstrated in a protected one-logical-qubit subspace of the block. This protocol-specific subsystem use is narrower than arbitrary two-logical-qubit fault tolerance.

    References: takita2017experimental

  • IBM five-qubit superconducting processors demonstrated

    Sampling, coherent-parity-check encoding, and logical randomized benchmarking experiments used the block with postselection. Their reported benefits belong to the measured circuits and devices, not to an asymptotic threshold of this fixed code.

    References: vuillot2018error, roffe2018protecting, harper2019faulttolerant

  • superconducting magic-state preparation circuit demonstrated

    An encoded CZ resource state was prepared in this block before a code-deformation and teleportation step moved the resource into a one-logical-qubit code. The beyond-break-even result belongs to that full preparation and transfer protocol.

    References: gupta2024encoding
