# Why cyclic HGP is exactly the Toric matrix

## The two periodic factors

Let `C_L` be the `L`-by-`L` binary matrix

```text
(C_L)[i,j] = 1  when j=i or j=i-1 mod L,
             0  otherwise.
```

Each row checks equality of two neighboring bits on a cycle. Unlike the open
repetition parity map, `C_L` is square. It has rank `L-1`, a one-dimensional
kernel generated by the all-one vector, and a one-dimensional transpose
kernel for the same reason.

Use `H1=C_L` for the horizontal direction and `H2=C_L^T` for the vertical
direction. The transpose is not decoration: it aligns the repository's
`x-major, then y` index convention with the chosen orientation of vertical
edges.

## Expand the HGP blocks

For `H1` of shape `m1`-by-`n1` and `H2` of shape `m2`-by-`n2`, the Atlas HGP
convention is

```text
HX = [ H1 ⊗ I_n2       | I_m1 ⊗ H2^T ]
HZ = [ I_n1 ⊗ H2       | H1^T ⊗ I_m2 ].
```

With the square cyclic factors, both qubit blocks have size `L²`. Identify the
first block with horizontal edges `h(x,y)` and the second with vertical edges
`w(x,y)`.

A row `(x,y)` of `HX` touches horizontal edges `h(x,y)` and `h(x-1,y)` through
`H1`, plus vertical edges `w(x,y)` and `w(x,y-1)` through `H2^T`. This is
exactly the vertex star at `(x,y)`.

A row `(x,y)` of `HZ` touches horizontal edges `h(x,y)` and `h(x,y+1)`, plus
vertical edges `w(x,y)` and `w(x+1,y)`. This is exactly the face boundary used
by the constructor. No row operation or qubit permutation is needed: under
the stated orientation, the matrices are byte-for-byte equal.

## Why there are two logical sectors

The HGP logical-dimension formula is

```text
k = (n1-r1)(n2-r2) + (m1-r1)(m2-r2).
```

For square cyclic factors, every `ni` and `mi` equals `L` and every `ri`
equals `L-1`. The kernel-kernel term contributes one logical sector, and the
transpose-kernel term contributes another. Their geometric representatives
are the two independent winding directions.

For the open repetition matrix used by the Surface entry, `m=L-1`, `n=L`, and
the map has full row rank. Its ordinary kernel has dimension one but its
transpose kernel has dimension zero. HGP of two open factors therefore keeps
only the first term and encodes one logical qubit. This is the algebraic form
of the boundary-condition difference.

## Why the card has no structured construction recipe

The repository's HGP operation accepts arbitrary binary factor matrices, but
the current structured generator vocabulary exposes only the open
`rep_parity` generator. Pretending that generator were cyclic would replay the
Surface matrix, not Toric. T144 is not authorized to add a shared generator or
construction API.

The card therefore carries a typed `product_of: hgp` scientific relation and a
derived cyclic-HGP view, while `reproduce.py` constructs `C_L` locally and
asserts exact equality for several sizes. This is executable provenance
without a false structured recipe. A later shared-infrastructure task may add
a cyclic generator and then migrate the result into a `construction:` facet.

## Reproduction checks

For `L=2,3,4`, `reproduce.py` asserts all of the following:

- `rank(C_L)=L-1` and both kernel dimensions are one;
- the HGP output has the constructor's exact `HX` and `HZ` arrays;
- the HGP dimension formula returns two;
- open `rep_parity(L)` has no transpose-kernel sector and its self-HGP has one
  logical qubit; and
- CSS commutation holds before any logical interpretation is attached.

Primary sources: `tillich2009quantum` for the HGP formula and
`kovalev2012improved` for the periodic Toric/HGP lineage.
