# Honeycomb Floquet code

## Essence

**The object.** Put one qubit on every vertex of a honeycomb torus. Every edge
has a direction label `x`, `y`, or `z` and carries the corresponding two-qubit
measurement `XX`, `YY`, or `ZZ`. A proper three-coloring of the hexagons gives
a second, independent edge label: the color missing from the two faces beside
that edge. At round `r`, measure all edges with missing color `r`. Those edges
form a perfect matching, so the measurements within one round commute even
though adjacent edge operators from different rounds anticommute.

That ordered, period-three measurement schedule is the code. The edge
operators are not a static stabilizer group. Instead, past outcomes define an
instantaneous stabilizer group (ISG) that is updated whenever the next
matching is measured. On the shipped `3 x 3` torus, the constructor tracks the
startup ranks

`9, 12, 14, 16, 16, 16, ...`

after successive rounds. The steady rank is `16` on `18` qubits, so two
logical qubits survive indefinitely. The reproduction script recomputes this
profile, the two-logical-qubit `6 x 6` instance, every perfect matching, and
the plaquette backbone; see the
[schedule and ISG derivation](pedagogy/schedule-isg-cycle.md). The executable
transcript is shipped as `pedagogy/reproduce.py`.

**The invariant.** Multiply the six directional edge operators around one
hexagon. At each vertex two different Pauli letters meet, leaving the third;
the product is a weight-six plaquette Pauli. It commutes with every edge
measurement and therefore survives all three rounds. On the `3 x 3` torus the
nine plaquette products have rank eight. Combining them with any steady-round
matching gives rank sixteen, with one shared dependency. This permanent
backbone plus one moving matching is the algebraic skeleton of the ISG.

**The post-struggle sentence.** Honeycomb protection comes from measuring
three incompatible perfect matchings in the right order: invariant plaquette
products remember the syndrome, while the currently measured matching
continually redraws a toric-code frame around two logical qubits.

## Design problem

Weight-two parity measurements are attractive because several hardware
architectures can implement them more directly than high-weight stabilizer
measurements. The obstruction is immediate. Two honeycomb edges meeting at a
vertex carry different Pauli letters, so their two-qubit operators
anticommute. No static stabilizer code can contain every edge operator.

The static-subsystem shortcut also fails. If all edge operators are declared
gauge generators, their center contains the plaquette products but the parent
subsystem group does not carry the two logical qubits generated by the
Floquet protocol. The information lives in the order of measurements and in
the outcome-dependent update rule, not in the common center alone. This is
the foundational distinction made by
[Hastings and Haah](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.02194).

The schedule solves three constraints simultaneously:

1. every round must be a perfect matching, so its measurements commute and
   touch every qubit exactly once;
2. the incoming matching must replace, rather than accumulate with, the
   anticommuting part of the previous ISG;
3. products of outcomes across time must expose stable plaquette information
   for a decoder.

The plaquette three-coloring supplies the needed matchings. Crucially, the
round label is not the edge-direction label. Each shipped round contains
equal numbers of `XX`, `YY`, and `ZZ` measurements. Grouping by direction
instead produces a valid-looking three-round schedule with the wrong
steady-state logical count. The
[wrong-schedule counterexample](pedagogy/schedule-isg-cycle.md#the-direction-grouped-counterexample)
is therefore part of the definition, not a coding-style warning.

The bargain is sharp: the code gains topological memory from weight-two
measurements, but syndrome information is spread over time. A decoder must
know the schedule, measurement circuit, detector definitions, and noise
normalization. A static parity-check matrix is insufficient.

## Derivation chain

**1. Separate direction from round.** For `L1 x L2` unit cells there are two
vertices per cell and hence `n = 2 L1 L2` qubits. The three geometric
directions determine Pauli type. A three-coloring of faces determines round.
For every edge, the two adjacent face colors are distinct; the unused color
is its round label.

**2. Prove that a round is measurable.** At a trivalent vertex, the three
incident edges separate the three pairs of neighboring face colors, so their
missing colors are all different. Exactly one edge of each round label meets
the vertex. Each round is therefore a disjoint union of edges covering every
qubit once: a perfect matching. The constructor checks this exact support
condition before returning a `Schedule`.

**3. Build the invariant plaquettes.** Let `W_p` be the product of the six
directional edge operators around face `p`. A boundary edge overlaps `W_p` at
two endpoints and contributes two local anticommutations. A non-boundary edge
touching the face meets it at one vertex, but `W_p` carries the same Pauli
letter there as that outward edge. Both cases commute. Thus every `W_p`
commutes with every measurement in every round. The
[plaquette calculation](pedagogy/schedule-isg-cycle.md#the-rank-eight-plaquette-backbone)
gives the explicit `3 x 3` words and the single global dependency.

**4. Update the ISG rather than append checks.** Suppose an incoming measured
Pauli anticommutes with old ISG generators. Choose one such generator as a
pivot, multiply it into the other anticommuting generators so they commute
with the measurement, and replace the pivot by the measured Pauli. This
preserves mutual commutation and is exactly the sign-free rule implemented by
`ISGTracker.measure`. After startup, each round replaces the moving matching
while the plaquette subgroup remains.

**5. Count what survives the whole sequence.** The static snapshot returned
by `constructor.py` has rank sixteen and therefore `k = 18 - 16 = 2`.
Independently, the schedule verifier evolves full periods until the rank
profile repeats and computes `dynamic_k = n - periodic_rank = 2`. Equality of
the two counts is a consistency check. A wrong schedule can have a plausible
snapshot at one time and a different long-time count, so the Atlas records
both properties.

**6. Contract the latest matching.** Fuse the two physical qubits on each
just-measured edge into one effective qubit. Under a depth-one local Clifford
change of basis, the moving checks and plaquette products become star and
plaquette stabilizers of a coarser toric lattice. This explains two torus
logical qubits at each steady time. The map changes from round to round,
which is why the full protocol carries a nontrivial anyon automorphism rather
than being one frozen toric code. The full dictionary lives on the
[instantaneous toric-frame page](pedagogy/toric-frame-automorphism.md).

**7. Turn outcomes into detectors.** An individual edge outcome is random
gauge information. A plaquette value is inferred from a prescribed product
of outcomes gathered at different times; comparing successive inferred
values produces a detection event. Physical faults become edges or
hyperedges in a spacetime detector model. The
[detector and distance page](pedagogy/spacetime-detectors-distance.md)
separates this circuit fault distance from any static snapshot distance.

## Multiple pictures

- **Schedule / ISG.** This is the executable Atlas representation. It answers
  which operators are measured, in what order, and how many logical qubits
  survive the repeated update. The landing-page explorer reads the exported
  schedule directly. Its constructor-derived coordinates form an equal-edge
  honeycomb in one periodic fundamental domain. Matching teal or violet arrows
  identify opposite sides, so a measurement cut by the displayed boundary is
  still one local edge on the torus rather than a long edge across the patch.

- **Invariant plaquettes plus a moving matching.** At a fixed steady time, the
  rank-eight plaquette subgroup is permanent and one matching supplies the
  changing local frame. This is the shortest algebraic explanation of why
  incompatible edge checks do not simply measure away the code.

- **Instantaneous toric code.** Contracting the most recent matching exposes a
  toric stabilizer group. This picture explains the two logical qubits and
  winding strings, but it does not include measurement faults or time ordering.
  See the [toric-frame dictionary](pedagogy/toric-frame-automorphism.md).

- **Spacetime detector complex.** Outcomes live on measurement events, while
  detectors relate events at different times. An error is classified by its
  detector boundary and logical action in spacetime. This is the correct
  picture for matching, timelike distance, leakage, and circuit thresholds;
  see the [decoder ledger](pedagogy/decoding-circuit-ledger.md).

- **Majorana / parton gauge theory.** Kitaev's representation writes each spin
  using four Majoranas and a local parity constraint. A directional Pauli edge
  factors into a `Z2` gauge link and a matter-Majorana pairing. Plaquette
  products become gauge fluxes; changing rounds re-pairs matter Majoranas.
  The exact correspondences and their limits are on the
  [representation dictionary](pedagogy/representation-dictionary.md).

- **Anyon automorphism and measurement QCA.** The sequence transports the
  instantaneous toric order and exchanges electric and magnetic labels after
  a period. On a boundary, the same protocol is a measurement quantum cellular
  automaton with information flow not visible in bulk rank. This picture is
  best for logical operations and boundary anomalies.

- **ZX spacetime.** A ZX network treats preparation, parity measurement,
  fusion, and Floquet schedules as alternative spacetime slicings related by
  local rewrites. It is useful for translating protocol structure, but it
  does not make two implementations share a noise model.

## Why these parameters

**Why `n = 2 L1 L2`.** The chosen periodic cell contains one `A` and one `B`
vertex. `constructor.py` enumerates exactly `L1 L2` cells and creates three
edges per cell, giving a trivalent graph with `2 L1 L2` vertices.

**Why both lengths are multiples of three.** The dual graph of honeycomb faces
is triangular. In the constructor's torus convention, the coloring
`c(i,j) = i - j mod 3` must return to itself around both periodic handles.
The two winding conditions require `L1 = 0 mod 3` and `L2 = 0 mod 3`.
The reproduction script exhausts all dimensions from two through nine and
confirms that the constraint solver finds a proper coloring exactly for those
pairs. Invalid sizes fail loudly instead of returning a different schedule.

**Why period three.** Three face colors produce three missing-color edge
classes, and every vertex meets one edge from each class. Fewer classes cannot
partition all three incident directions into perfect matchings while retaining
the same plaquette-color construction.

**Why `k = dynamic_k = 2` on the torus.** Nine plaquettes have rank eight.
At a steady time, the current matching has rank nine, and its span intersects
the plaquette span in one global relation. The ISG rank is therefore
`8 + 9 - 1 = 16`; subtracting from eighteen leaves two. The `6 x 6` instance
independently reaches rank seventy on seventy-two qubits. These are recomputed
facts, not parameter formulas copied from a paper.

**Why no `d` appears in `instances.yaml`.** The exact distance of the frozen
ISG is not the operational distance of a noisy periodic measurement protocol.
Floquet fault distance minimizes over undetectable, non-benign spacetime error
histories and depends on the circuit and observation window. The present
verifier has no such computation, so the entry leaves `d` unknown rather than
mislabeling a snapshot distance. The formal gap is explained on the
[distance page](pedagogy/spacetime-detectors-distance.md).

## Failure modes

- **Grouping by Pauli direction.** Measuring every `XX` edge, then every `YY`
  edge, then every `ZZ` edge also gives three perfect matchings and preserves
  the plaquette products. It is nevertheless a different dynamical code. The
  reproduction anti-test finds `dynamic_k = 20` on its `6 x 6` torus, while
  the plaquette-color schedule has `dynamic_k = 2`. Local commutation and a
  surviving center are not sufficient certificates.

- **Reading distance from one frame.** The instantaneous toric equivalence
  does not turn circuit faults into a static toric syndrome. Measurement
  errors, time boundaries, and benign spacetime deformations are missing from
  that snapshot. Thresholds and fault distances must name a circuit and
  detector model.

- **Transplanting thresholds.** The six-step CNOT circuit, native entangling
  measurement model, planar boundary circuits, Majorana parity model, and
  photonic loss model have different locations and correlations. Their
  thresholds are not interchangeable properties of `card.yaml`'s
  `CheckMatrix`; the [quantitative ledger](pedagogy/decoding-circuit-ledger.md)
  keeps each number attached to its experiment.

- **Confusing the Kitaev Hamiltonian with this code.** The static honeycomb
  spin model supplies the directional operators and Majorana language. Its
  ground-state phase diagram is not a realization of a repeated
  measurement-only memory.

- **Treating every honeycomb-layout code as this schedule.** CSS honeycomb,
  X3Z3, hyperbolic, qudit, twist-network, and higher-dimensional descendants
  alter independent axes: Pauli pattern, geometry, boundaries, or circuit.
  The [variant map](pedagogy/boundaries-variants.md) states which claims do
  and do not transfer.

- **Ignoring startup and readout.** The steady ISG emerges only after
  measurements establish enough outcome constraints. A real memory experiment
  also needs state preparation, detector-boundary conventions, final logical
  readout, and a decoder that handles both temporal ends.

## Operations story

The unmodified memory schedule already performs a logical-frame action. In
the instantaneous toric description, one period exchanges electric and
magnetic anyon labels. A winding representative that is electric in one frame
must therefore be redressed and interpreted as magnetic in the next. Two
periods restore the anyon label, although outcome-dependent Pauli-frame updates
still have to be tracked. This is a measurement-based topological Clifford
automorphism, not a transversal gate on a fixed block.

More elaborate operations change geometry or schedule:

- condensation lines and twist endpoints can be created, moved, fused, and
  braided while retaining two-body measurements;
- a non-orientable cross-cap geometry makes one period act as logical `HZ` on
  its encoded qubit, with folding providing a bilayer implementation;
- general automorphism-code and fixed-point-circuit frameworks explain how
  protected paths act on anyon labels.

These mechanisms and their assumptions are separated on the
[logical-operations page](pedagogy/logical-operations.md). The six-lens sweep
searched through 2026-07-26 and found no complete universal, fully benchmarked
gate set for the unmodified planar Hastings-Haah memory. Results for Floquet
color codes or Floquet-Bacon-Shor are neighboring protocols, not native
honeycomb operations.

## Litmus questions

**Q1:** Why do repeated incompatible measurements not accumulate until
`k = 0`? **A:** An incoming measurement is not blindly appended to the ISG.
When it anticommutes with old generators, the update rule replaces one pivot
and multiplies that pivot into the other anticommuting rows. The rank-eight
plaquette subgroup survives, while the moving matching is exchanged for the
new one. Direct evolution reaches rank sixteen and then repeats, leaving two
logical qubits.

**Q2:** What are the two different labels on an edge? **A:** Its geometric
direction chooses the Pauli letter `X`, `Y`, or `Z`. The missing color of its
two adjacent plaquettes chooses measurement round `0`, `1`, or `2`. Confusing
these labels produces the direction-grouped counterexample. Every correct
round in the shipped `3 x 3` schedule contains three measurements of each
Pauli type.

**Q3:** Why is `dynamic_k` needed when the snapshot already reports `k = 2`?
**A:** Snapshot `k` counts the centralizer quotient of one frozen ISG.
`dynamic_k` first evolves the repeated schedule to its periodic rank and asks
how many qubits survive the entire sequence. Their agreement certifies this
constructor; wrong schedules can make the two notions diverge or stabilize at
the wrong family scaling.

**Q4:** Can the toric-code distance be quoted as the Honeycomb fault distance?
**A:** No. The local Clifford contraction describes an instantaneous
stabilizer frame. Circuit fault distance classifies complete spacetime error
histories modulo benign errors, including faulty measurements and temporal
boundaries. Until that detector circuit is specified and analyzed, the Atlas
leaves `d` unclaimed.

**Q5:** What did the hardware evidence establish as of the 2026-07-26
literature sweep? **A:** The literal IBM experiment measured Honeycomb
plaquette stabilizers and reported syndrome-change behavior, but it did not
demonstrate a repeatedly protected logical memory or distance scaling.
Majorana, photonic, fabrication-defect, and dynamic-circuit results in the
sweep are proposals or simulations. The
[realization ledger](pedagogy/realization-ledger.md) keeps those boundaries
explicit.
